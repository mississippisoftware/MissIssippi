# Founding Story

> **Purpose:** The 30-second story of why this product exists. Written by the GTM Strategist at the end of Phase 1 kickoff. Kept fresh for future marketing, pitches, landing pages, and sales conversations. Do not polish this into corporate-speak — the point is that it's authentic operator language.

---

## The Moment

We run a wholesale girls' apparel brand. Twice a year we do a show with samples on the rack. Our salespeople stand at the booth with customers, write orders on paper, and hand the paper to the office. The office types those paper orders into Excel. Then someone consolidates the Excel into manufacturing POs. Then, when it's time to ship, someone manually types invoices and pick tickets into QuickBooks — because none of the earlier work flows into QuickBooks automatically.

Every piece of information is entered three times. Every entry is a chance to make a mistake. Every mistake costs money or a customer.

---

## The Problem (In One Sentence)

Small apparel wholesalers run their businesses on paper, Excel, and QuickBooks duct-taped together, because the real apparel tools (Cin7, NetSuite, big ERPs) are built for companies ten times their size.

---

## The Insight

**"Enter information once."** When a salesperson takes an order at the show, that single entry should flow downstream — into the consolidation report that drives manufacturing POs, into the pick ticket when stock arrives, into the invoice when the order ships, into the A/R aging when the customer pays. Paper and Excel break the chain. We fix the chain.

---

## Why We Can Build It

Because we're the customer. I run the business this software is for. I know that style × color × size × season is how apparel actually works — not a hack bolted onto a generic product catalog. I know that the show-floor UX has to be fast because a customer is standing there. I know that wholesale pricing and retail pricing are different conversations. I'm not guessing what small apparel brands need — I'm solving my own problem in public, and when it works for me it will work for them.

---

## What This Is (And Isn't)

- **Is:** A wholesale-first apparel business platform. Inventory, orders, purchasing, invoicing, basic A/R. Built like a single tool, not six integrations.
- **Isn't:** A general ERP. Not Shopify. Not QuickBooks. Not enterprise. Not trying to be everything.

---

## The Wedge (Year 2+ Go-to-Market)

When we pitch this to another small apparel brand, the first question we ask is: *"How many times do you type the same customer's order between the show and the shipment?"*

If the answer is zero, we're not their fit.

If the answer is three, we are.
