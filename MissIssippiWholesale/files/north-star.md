# North Star

> **Purpose:** The single vision statement this project serves. Every decision gets checked against this. If something doesn't serve the North Star, it goes in the Parking Lot — not the to-do list.

---

## Vision Statement

A web-based business management platform for small girls' apparel wholesalers, built by an operator who runs one — matched to how apparel actually works (style × color × size × season, wholesale and retail channels, show-floor order entry) rather than retrofitted from a generic retail tool.

**Year 1 (now):** A platform *I* use to run *my* wholesale girls' apparel business end-to-end — from taking orders at a show, to placing POs with manufacturers, to receiving inventory, to shipping and invoicing, to basic A/R. Kills the current paper → Excel → QuickBooks re-entry pipeline.

**Year 2+:** A product other small girls' apparel wholesalers can buy, because I've already solved their problem for myself.

---

## Who This Is For

### Primary user (Year 1): Me

- Solo operator of a wholesale girls' apparel brand
- Seasonal drops (spring + fall); ~2,500 SKUs per season; ~400 orders per season
- Wholesale-only today; retail/e-commerce planned post-Year-1
- Sells through wholesale accounts, with salespeople taking orders on paper today
- Currently uses: this inventory app + Excel + QuickBooks (the last two are the pain)

### Target customer (Year 2+): Other small girls' apparel wholesalers

- Small enough that general-purpose ERPs are overkill
- Large enough to outgrow spreadsheets
- Apparel-shaped inventory (style/color/size/season)
- Wholesale-first or wholesale-and-retail hybrid
- Running on QuickBooks + Excel today (same pain I had)

---

## What Makes This Different

1. **Built by an apparel operator.** Style → color → SKU is first-class, not a hack on top of a generic product table. Seasons, collections, size runs, wholesale/retail pricing are real concepts, not improvisations.
2. **"Enter information once."** Show-floor order entry feeds POs, pick orders, invoices, and A/R without re-keying. The paper → Excel → QB pipeline goes away.
3. **Operator-grade polish.** I'm building the tool I'd want to use for 8 hours a day. It has to feel fast and quiet, not busy.

*(More differentiators to be added once Phase 3 and 4 ship and we have evidence of what actually matters most to buyers.)*

---

## What This Is NOT

- Not a general-purpose ERP.
- Not for large enterprises.
- Not competing with Shopify on storefront features (integration, not replacement).
- Not replacing QuickBooks for deep accounting (basic A/R and P&L only; export to QB if needed).
- Not a manufacturing / BOM / production-planning system.
- Not a payroll or HR system.
- Not a marketing automation platform.
- Not a mobile-phone-first tool (tablet-landscape and desktop for now).

---

## Review Cadence

The North Star is reviewed at every phase transition. It can change — but only deliberately, with a logged decision in `decisions-log.md`, not by drift.
